Government Schemes

Discover and apply for agricultural subsidies and financial assistance programs.

PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi provides minimum income support of ₹6,000 per year directly to farmer accounts.

PM Fasal Bima Yojana

A comprehensive crop insurance scheme safeguarding against unseasonal rains and non-preventable natural risks.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Access adequate and timely credit support from the banking system via flexible single-window loans.

Soil Health Card

State governments issue soil assessment cards assessing nutrient deficiencies to promote balanced fertilizer use.

e-NAM Portal

A pan-India electronic trading portal networking existing APMC mandis to create a unified national market.

PKVY Organic Farming

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana promotes organic farming through a cluster approach with financial assistance.
